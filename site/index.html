What it does

Your agents keep their terminals. You keep one reflex.

Reflex never wraps your agents in an SDK or a chat API. They run in their own TUIs inside herdr, on the plans you already pay for. Reflex just knows where they are.

◐The edge of your screen

A small tab grows from the right edge while agents work — a spinning ring for each, amber when one is waiting on you, green when done. Hover it for the rail.

✦⌥⌥ to ask

Double-tap Option and type. A few words find a session; a sentence becomes a request, and Jev ranks where it should go — a live session, a folder, an earlier conversation.

1Questions, answered anywhere

Every question an agent stops on — numbered options, multi-select, “trust this folder?” — becomes a card you answer with 1, 2, 3, from the rail, the inbox or your phone.

▯Take it with you

Scan a code that works once. Your phone gets your agents, their questions and a live terminal you can type into — over Tailscale, private and from anywhere.

↺Nothing is lost

Earlier Claude, pi and Codex sessions come back with a keystroke. Background sessions and background shells count as work until they are really done.

☕Awake while they work

Your Mac doesn't idle-sleep while an agent is working — and lets go on battery before it runs low.

How it routes

A System 1 model, not a chat agent at the helm.

No LLM rewrites your words. Reflex asks Jev typed questions over candidates it built itself, and gets back keys and probabilities in about a second.

Candidates, built deterministically

Your live sessions (their folder, state and last lines), the git repos under your roots, and your earlier conversations — ranked so Jev only ever chooses among a few.

One typed call

Which session? Which folder? Is this a side question? Does it continue earlier work? Plus where your last requests went, so “also add tests for that” lands right.

Sure, or it asks

When Jev is sure, your words go — verbatim. When it isn't, its best guess is highlighted for you to confirm. Those rules are laws, machine-checked in Bend.
