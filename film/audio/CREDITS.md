# The launch film's score: where every sound comes from

Every recording is **CC0 1.0** (public domain): no attribution is required, but here they are.
Nothing is synthesized as music; the samples are only trimmed, re-pitched (varispeed), mixed and
mastered. `node film/audio/fetch.mjs` downloads them into `film/audio/samples/` (not committed).

| Claps | VCSL | `Idiophones/Struck Idiophones/Claps` |
